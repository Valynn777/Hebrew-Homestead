# Hebrew Homestead Content Guidelines

## Core Perspective

Hebrew Homestead is a peaceful Torah-observant click-based homestead stewardship game.

The game assumes that Yehovah’s instructions are good, wise, and still relevant. Yeshua is the Word made flesh and did not abolish or change Yehovah’s commandments. The game does not debate Torah observance; it treats obedience, stewardship, rest, clean living, and gratitude as part of the natural order of the world.

The tone should remain warm, peaceful, instructional, and Scripture-centered.

## Clean Food Rules

All food, farming, cooking, animal, and recipe systems must remain biblically clean.

Every edible item should include a data field like:

```js
cleanStatus: "clean"
```

If an item is questionable, unreviewed, or unclassified, it must not be edible, craftable as food, or used as a food reward until reviewed.

Do not include as food, livestock, recipes, rewards, or ingredients:

- Pork or pig-related products
- Shellfish
- Unclean birds
- Blood
- Carrion or animals that died on their own
- Unclean animals used as farm food products
- Fantasy foods that conflict with clean food rules

Allowed food categories:

- Grains
- Fruits
- Vegetables
- Legumes
- Herbs
- Eggs
- Milk
- Honey
- Clean animal products, if added carefully later

Meat should be avoided unless specifically requested and reviewed against the clean-food rules.

Clean wild game may be added when specifically requested and reviewed. Deer may be represented as clean wild game, with venison handled as a clean food only when prepared without blood and framed as careful stewardship rather than sport or combat.

Fish systems must only use fish that meet the biblical clean-food category of fins and scales. Generic or unreviewed fish should not be edible.

Clean livestock and reviewed clean birds may provide eggs, milk, wool, hides, feathers, and meat. Any meat item must be from a reviewed clean animal, prepared without blood, and presented soberly as stewardship rather than combat, cruelty, or spectacle.

## Animals

Allowed future farm animals may include:

- Chickens
- Sheep
- Goats
- Cattle
- Bees

Do not add pigs, rabbits, camels, horses, donkeys, shellfish, or unclean birds as food animals.

Purchasing and selling animals should remain fictional in-game trade. Do not connect to real animal sales, real money, external stores, or live marketplaces.

## Sabbath

Sabbath should be part of the weekly rhythm.

The player works six days and rests on the seventh.

Sabbath should not feel like punishment. It should feel like trust, order, delight, preparation, and restoration.

On the sixth day, include a Sabbath preparation quest.

Possible preparation goals:

- Prepare clean meals
- Gather water
- Feed and water animals
- Clean/tidy the cottage
- Put tools away
- Finish or pause active crafting
- Set aside a Sabbath basket
- Read a preparation journal entry

Once preparation goals are met, the player may choose:

“Enter Sabbath Rest”

During Sabbath, pause ordinary labor:

- Hoeing
- Planting
- Chopping
- Mining
- Building
- Crafting
- Profit-focused harvesting

Allow peaceful or necessary actions:

- Journal reading
- Prayer/reflection
- Eating prepared food
- Walking
- Basic animal care
- Basic crop care if needed as mercy/necessity

After Sabbath ends, give a “Shalom Rest” boost.

Possible boost effects:

- Restore stamina
- Restore energy
- Gentle next-day focus benefit
- Unlock Scripture reflection

For version 1:

- Preparation Day is the sixth day of the week.
- Sabbath is the seventh day.
- Ordinary labor pauses during Sabbath: planting, chopping, heavy gathering, ordering, building, ordinary harvesting, and crafting.
- Peaceful navigation, journal reading, eating prepared clean food, reflection, and basic mercy or necessity care such as watering crops may remain available.
- Sabbath should be presented as blessing, trust, delight, preparation, and restoration.

Planned biblical systems may include:

- Clean food system
- Firstfruits basket
- Gleaning portion
- Honest measures
- Soil rest
- Crop rotation
- Appointed times calendar
- Sukkot preparation and rejoicing
- Prayer/reflection

- Cleanliness and food preparation

## Tone Rules

Do not make the game argumentative.

Do not frame Torah observance as a debate.

Do not add denominational commentary.

Do not present obedience as cold rule-keeping.

Do present Yehovah’s instructions as wise, loving, ordered, and good.

Do connect work, rest, food, farming, and stewardship to gratitude and faithfulness.

## Herbalism and Health Notes

Herbalism content should be educational and cautious.

Use wording such as:

- Traditionally used for
- Commonly used to support
- Historically prepared as
- May support
- Use caution

Avoid wording such as:

- Cures
- Treats
- Guaranteed to heal
- Replaces medical care

Include safety cautions when appropriate.

Herbal content must not diagnose illness, prescribe treatment, promise healing, or replace professional medical care. Prefer short historical or traditional notes with cautious wording.

## What Not to Include

- Multiplayer, login systems, online databases, or AI features inside the game
- Denominational disputes or commentary
- Debate over Torah observance
- Unclean food, livestock, ingredients, recipes, rewards, or animal products
- Medical claims about herbs
- Combat-centered systems or harsh survival pressure
