Future local assets may also include:

- Gentle interface icons
- Scene transition details
- Crop bed detail overlays
- Additional field and fence-gate artwork
- Barn animal and care overlays
- Forest resource overlays for trees, stones, fishing, and clean wild game
- Sabbath area details
- Sound effects and quiet music

All assets should support the peaceful, Scripture-centered tone of Hebrew Homestead and must avoid unclean food or animal imagery used as edible items, livestock, recipes, or rewards.
